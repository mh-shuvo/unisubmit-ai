**Theme:** T2

## Symptom

A margin rule written about the chapter-heading area was applied to every page
in the document, so a compliant document failed on pages the rule never
concerned.

## Root cause

The same scope leak as [DEF-010](DEF-010.md), on margins and line spacing:
a quantity measured per page was compared against a requirement written about
one area of the document.

## Fix

Scope both to the target. Where a sub-area margin cannot be isolated at
whole-page level, report `NOT VERIFIED` with the reason rather than failing
pages the rule does not govern. Section-scoped spacing is likewise not decided
from page medians.

## Verified

Compliant pages stop failing area-scoped rules, and the rules that cannot be
isolated say so instead.

## Tests

`tests/test_format_validation_rules.py :: SectionScoping.test_named_area_margin_is_not_a_document_failure`
and `test_section_scoped_spacing_is_not_verified_from_page_medians`, with
`test_document_margin_still_fails_on_intrusion` guarding the document-wide case.

## For the thesis

T2, and it shows where T2 meets T3. Not every scope leak can be repaired by
narrowing the measurement: some sub-area requirements cannot be isolated from a
page raster at all, and for those the only honest result is `NOT VERIFIED`. The
limits of what the deterministic layer can measure are a finding in their own
right, not a gap to apologise for.
