## Statement

`num_ctx` is 16,384 tokens and `num_predict` is 4,096. Every semantic
validation decision has to fit inside that: the instructions, the JSON schema,
the shared retrieval context, the per-rule evidence and the model's answer for
every rule in the batch.

`SEMANTIC_BATCH_SIZE = 12` and `SECTION_EVIDENCE_CHARS = 3000` are not tuning
preferences. They are the largest values that keep a batch inside both budgets,
and they were derived rather than chosen.

## Consequence

A thesis cannot be validated in one pass. Requirements are decided twelve at a
time against bounded evidence, so the model never sees the whole document while
deciding any one requirement, and evidence beyond 3,000 characters for a given
rule is not considered unless the shared full-text fallback supplies it.

A guideline with substantially more requirements costs proportionally more
calls, and wall-clock time scales with it. This interacts with
[LIM-003](LIM-003.md): more batches means the model is held for longer.

## Why it is accepted

Running a larger context locally is a hardware decision, not a code change.
The constraint is recorded so that results are read in the light of it rather
than being presented as though the model had the whole document in view.

## For the thesis

T4. This is a threat to validity for every semantic result the system reports,
and it should be stated as one. Bounded evidence means a `NOT VERIFIED` can mean
the evidence was absent or merely outside the window, and the system cannot
currently distinguish those two. Distinguishing them would be worthwhile future
work.
