## Statement

Both episode documents assert that every defect was reproduced against the live
engine before and after the change, and cite the artefacts that did it. Those
artefacts are not in the repository.

`unsatisfiable_rules_defects_and_fixes.md` references
`repro_spacing.py`, `probe_threshold.py`, `probe_isolate.py`,
`repro_headings.py`, `repro_bold.py` and `check.py` under
`resources/sample-thesis/AI Tools for Improving Hospital Patient Care/REVISED5_source/`.
There is no `resources/` directory.

`validation_defects_and_fixes.md` states outright that the sample PDFs it
refers to, including `submission_1_v2_80120022.pdf`, were deleted after testing.

## Consequence

No quantitative claim in either document can currently be replayed. The
measurements that make the strongest case in the register, the 1.41 to 1.63 em
block threshold across nine font sizes in [DEF-017](DEF-017.md), the 18.75 pt
against 20.64 pt gap, the 39 PASS to 40 PASS and 97.9% to 100% result, all rest
on artefacts a reader cannot obtain.

The 46 unit tests do guard the behaviour, which is the stronger guarantee for
the code. They do not reproduce the measurements, which is what the thesis
quotes.

## What closing it requires

1. Commit the probe and reproduction scripts. They are small, they have no
   dependencies beyond PyMuPDF, and they are what makes the numbers checkable.
2. Commit one redistributable sample document, or a generator that produces an
   equivalent one, so the before-and-after numbers can be regenerated.
3. Record the guideline version and job number alongside each measurement, as
   [`CONVENTIONS.md`](CONVENTIONS.md) now requires for new entries.

## For the thesis

T6, and the entry with the most direct bearing on the submission itself. An
examiner reading "each defect was reproduced against the live engine" is
entitled to ask for the reproduction. This is the one open item worth closing
before the measurements are quoted in the text.
