## Symptom

Any numbered heading took two mandatory FAILs that no student could clear, and
four rules targeting `Third Level Heading` read `NOT VERIFIED`.

## Root cause

All three recognised heading targets entered one branch that discarded the
target and returned an identical list of lines. Rule 25 then required those
lines to be 14 pt and rule 29 required the same lines to be 12 pt. With
tolerance 0.7 the accepted windows are 13.3 to 14.7 and 11.3 to 12.7, which do
not overlap, so a heading at 12 pt failed one rule, at 14 pt the other, and at
13 pt both.

Two further faults sat in the same function. It had no contents-page guard,
unlike `_find_actual_heading`, so contents entries beginning `Chapter N` or
`1.1` were collected as headings and failed the centring rule even when every
real chapter heading was centred. And `Third Level Heading` matched nothing at
all, because it fell through to the substring branch and no line contains that
phrase.

## Fix

Give each heading level its own pattern, and skip contents pages for level
matches.

## Verified

A properly numbered document now passes 11 of the 12 heading rules, up from 12
`NOT VERIFIED`. The twelfth is [DEF-020](DEF-020.md), still open.

## Tests

`tests/test_format_validation_rules.py :: HeadingLevelSeparation`, seven cases
including `test_both_size_rules_pass_on_one_compliant_document` for the
contradiction itself and `test_contents_entries_are_not_headings` for the guard.
Confirmed red by reverting only this fix.

## For the thesis

T2. The most striking shape in the register: two individually reasonable rules
that were jointly unsatisfiable, not because the guideline was contradictory but
because the engine collapsed two distinct targets onto one set of lines. The
contradiction lived in the implementation, and only appeared when both rules
were evaluated against the same document. Single-rule testing cannot find this.
