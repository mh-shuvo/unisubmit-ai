## Symptom

A supervisor with a joint appointment in two departments cannot be added to
the second department's directory: the insert fails on the unique e-mail. Once
several universities share an installation, the second university cannot
create its own "Faculty of Engineering" or a department coded `CSE`.

## Root cause

`unique=True` on `Teacher.email` and on the names and codes of faculties,
institutes and departments. The rule the data needs is "unique within a unit"
or "unique within a university"; the constraint enforces "unique within the
installation".

## Reproduction

```
# as two different unit admins, add the same supervisor e-mail to each
# unit's directory: the second insert fails on teachers.email
```

## What closing it requires

Uniqueness per unit for supervisors and per university for unit names and
codes, after the `universities` table exists (task P2.3), through a migration
([DEF-039](DEF-039.md)).

## For the thesis

T8. Filed under `scope-leakage` by analogy: a rule about one area (a unit, a
university) is decided over the whole installation. The class was defined for
measurements; this is the same mistake in the data model, and the entry is
the place that judgement is recorded.
